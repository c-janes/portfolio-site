# Portfolio Site Plan

## Wireframe Layouts
- **Home Page (index.html):** 
  - Top: Header with Site Title & Shared Nav Bar
  - Middle: Hero section (`<main>`) with a welcome introduction and short bio paragraph
  - Bottom: Footer with copyright
- **Projects Page (projects.html):** 
  - Top: Shared Nav Bar
  - Middle: `<main>` section with a heading and individual `<article>` blocks for project highlights
  - Bottom: Footer
- **About & Contact Page (about.html):** 
  - Top: Shared Nav Bar
  - Middle: `<main>` section with background background info and a contact section
  - Bottom: Footer

## Design Choices
- **Color Palette:** Dark gray background (`#1e1e2e`), white text (`#cdd6f4`), and an accent teal or blue for links (`#89b4fa`).
- **Font Choices:** Sans-serif fonts like system-ui, Inter, or Roboto for a readable look.
